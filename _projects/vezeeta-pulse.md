Engineered a resilient Python/Scrapy crawler across 1,640+ directory pages, capturing 17,119 active doctor records with rate limiting and recovery protocols.

Analyzed data with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to surface geographic pricing variation and specialty shortages across 140 areas.

Directed the full lifecycle from raw extraction to an interactive Flask dashboard with a FAISS-powered semantic search engine.
